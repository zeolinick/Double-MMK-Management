Owner FAQ

Questions owners ask us

How much does property management cost?

Our full-service management fee is a simple percentage of collected rent, with a one-time lease-up fee for placing new tenants. You'll receive an exact quote in your free proposal — with no hidden charges.

How quickly can you rent my property?

Our average time to lease is about 21 days, depending on location, condition, and pricing. We'll give you an honest timeline during your rental analysis.

How are tenants screened?

Every applicant undergoes credit, background, eviction-history, employment, and income verification to ensure we place reliable, qualified residents.

When and how do I get paid?

Owner payouts are sent via direct deposit each month once rent is collected, along with a detailed statement you can access anytime in your owner portal.

Do you handle maintenance and repairs?

Yes. We coordinate all routine and emergency maintenance through our network of trusted, licensed Detroit contractors, with spending limits you control.

Resident FAQ

Common tenant questions

How do I pay my rent?

Rent is paid online through the Landlord Studio tenant portal. You can pay by bank transfer or card and set up automatic payments so you never miss a due date.

What counts as a maintenance emergency?

Emergencies include fire, flooding, gas leaks, no heat in freezing weather, or anything threatening safety. Call our 24/7 line at (313) 603-6064 immediately. Everything else can go through the request form.

How do I get access to the tenant portal?

When your lease begins, we send a Landlord Studio invitation to your email so you can set up your resident account. Lost your invite? Contact our office and we'll resend it.

How do I renew my lease?

We'll reach out before your lease ends with renewal options. You're also welcome to contact us anytime to discuss renewing early.
