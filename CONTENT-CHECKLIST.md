# What to send to finish the website

The site is built and polished. To turn it from a great template into
unmistakably **Double MMK's** site (and to switch the forms "on"), here's
everything we need from you. It's grouped by priority — Tier 1 is what
actually makes the site go live and feel real.

---

## ⭐ Tier 1 — Needed to launch

### 1. Turn the forms on (2 minutes)
Right now the contact + "Free Rental Analysis" forms show a thank-you
message but don't email anyone yet.
- Go to **web3forms.com**, enter **Doublemmkmanagement@gmail.com**, and it
  emails you an **access key** (a long code).
- Send Nick that key. That's it — leads then go straight to the Gmail inbox.

### 2. Real reviews (3–6)
We're currently using placeholder testimonials. Real ones build far more trust.
- Copy/paste **3–6 real client quotes** (Google reviews are perfect).
- For each: the **quote**, the person's **first name + last initial**, and a
  short tag (e.g. "Owner, 4 units" or "Single-family owner").
- If you have a **Google Business Profile**, send the link too.

### 3. Owner photo (high resolution)
- One clear, **high-resolution headshot of Mohamad** (at least **1000×1000px**,
  ideally larger). The one we have is low-res.

### 4. A few real property photos
Even 4–6 real photos beats the illustrations we're using now.
- **8–12 horizontal photos** total if possible, **at least 1600px wide**, JPG.
- Exteriors + a few nice interiors (kitchen, living room, bathroom).
- If they're tied to specific rentals, note the **address/neighborhood** for each.

### 5. Confirm the business facts
- **Office address** — a real street address, OR confirm we should keep
  "Serving Detroit & the metro area / by appointment."
- **License / registration number** (if you have one to display).
- **Exact business hours** (we currently show Mon–Fri 9–5).
- **Service area** — confirm the neighborhood list is right (add/remove any).

### 6. Confirm the numbers
We're showing these as round figures — confirm they're OK or give real ones:
- **500+ units managed**, **98% occupancy**, **21 days avg. to lease**,
  **4.9★ satisfaction**.
- **Pricing** on the Owners page: **50% of one month's rent** (lease-up) and
  **10% of monthly rent** (full management), with a **$100 per-door monthly
  minimum**; custom pricing for large portfolios & apartment buildings.
- **Rent calculator**: glance at a few estimates and tell us if the ranges
  feel right for your market (easy to fine-tune).
