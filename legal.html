Privacy, terms, accessibility, and our commitment to Fair Housing.

Privacy Policy

Double MMK Management LLC respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

When you submit a form on this site (rental analysis, contact, or maintenance request), we collect the details you provide — such as your name, email, phone number, and property information — so we can respond to your request.

How we use it

We use your information solely to communicate with you and provide our services. We do not sell your personal information. We may use trusted service providers (for example, our property management software, Landlord Studio) to deliver those services.
