01 — Leasing & Marketing

Fill vacancies fast, with the right tenants

We make your property shine and put it in front of every qualified renter in Detroit.

  • Professional photography and compelling listings
  • Syndication to Zillow, Apartments.com, and more
  • Prompt showings and applicant follow-up
  • Market-based rent pricing to reduce vacancy

What this means for you: fewer empty months, a qualified resident in place faster, and rent set right the first time.

02 — Tenant Screening & Placement

Quality residents who stay

The right tenant is the foundation of a profitable rental. We vet thoroughly so you don't have to worry.

  • Credit, background, and eviction checks
  • Income and employment verification
  • Rental history and reference review
  • Compliant, attorney-reviewed lease agreements

What this means for you: residents who pay on time and stay for years — fewer turnovers, fewer headaches, steadier income.

03 — Maintenance & Repairs

Around-the-clock property care

Happy tenants and protected value start with responsive, reliable maintenance.

  • 24/7 emergency maintenance line
  • Vetted, licensed local Detroit contractors
  • Online maintenance requests & tracking
  • Preventative upkeep and seasonal checks

What this means for you: small issues caught before they get expensive, tenants who feel cared for, and a property that holds its value.

04 — Rent Collection & Accounting

Dependable income, clear books

Powered by Landlord Studio, our rent and accounting tools keep your cash flow smooth and your records spotless.

  • Automated online rent payments
  • Proactive late-payment follow-up
  • Monthly owner statements & direct deposit
  • Year-end financials for tax time

What this means for you: rent that lands on time and books that are always tax-ready — so you can see exactly how your investment is performing.

05 — Inspections & Compliance

Stay protected and up to code

Detroit's rental rules are detailed — we keep your property certified and compliant.

  • Move-in and move-out inspections
  • Periodic condition assessments
  • City of Detroit rental compliance & certification
  • Documentation and photo records

What this means for you: no surprise violations or fines, and full documentation if a dispute ever comes up — your property stays protected.
