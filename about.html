Our Story

It started with unreturned phone calls.

I started Double MMK after watching too many Detroit owners deal with managers who wouldn’t return a call. Hidden fees, slow repairs, a different rep every month — the fix wasn’t complicated: answer the phone, tell the truth, do the work.

Today we manage homes across the city on that one promise — every door, one standard. Landlord Studio handles the rent and the statements; we handle everything a portal can’t.

A Note From Our Founder

Why I started Double MMK

When I started Double MMK Management, it wasn't to build the biggest management company in Detroit — it was to build one people could actually trust. I'd watched too many owners get stuck with management that didn't return calls, padded invoices, and treated their property like a number on a spreadsheet.

I knew it could be done better, the old-fashioned way: pick up the phone, tell the truth, and do the work. Every property we take on, I treat like my own. Every resident, like a neighbor. We pair that hands-on approach with modern tools so owners always know what's happening — and tenants always have a real way to reach us.

Detroit is home. If you own property here, I'd be glad to show you what management done right feels like.

Mohamad Kahala

Owner, Double MMK Management LLC
