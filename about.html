Our Story

Management with a personal touch

Double MMK Management was founded to give Detroit property owners something many big firms forget: a partner who actually answers the phone. We saw owners frustrated by hidden fees, slow maintenance, and impersonal service — and we set out to do it better.

Today we manage homes across Detroit with a simple promise: treat every property like our own and every resident like a neighbor. We pair modern tools like Landlord Studio with old-fashioned, hands-on care.
